Academic projects

Econometrics and risk modelling, in R and Python.

  • Digital Trade and Geopolitical Regime Divergence A gravity-model test of whether data localization and market-regime divergence hold back bilateral trade once distance, size and shared borders are accounted for. PPMLHDFE · 6,960 bilateral pairs · R
  • Continuous-Time FX Volatility Modeling An extended Ornstein–Uhlenbeck model with the US 10-year yield in the drift, ranking the ASEAN-5 currencies by how strongly they respond to US monetary shocks. Extended OU SDE · IDR THB SGD MYR PHP · Python
  • Trade Policy Uncertainty and Export Volatility How US trade policy uncertainty moves Indonesian exports in the long run, and how long the volatility it creates persists. VECM-GARCH · 128 monthly observations · R
  • Credit Risk and Firm Fundamentals A Bharath–Shumway distance-to-default model for IDX-listed firms, benchmarked against gradient boosting. Distance to default · LightGBM · Python Ongoing
  • Tail Risk in Energy Commodities Score-driven GAS/GPD tail models for WTI, RBOB and Henry Hub futures, with NASA storm tracks and temperature reanalysis as drivers of tail behaviour. GAS/DCS + GPD · satellite covariates · Python Null result
